\begin{abstract}
We seek one policy per user, trained first from that user's thoughts,
conversations, and task history, then updated from later interactions.  OPPU
is the closest offline method: it fits one private adapter from one user's
history.  SPRInG is the closest continual method: it updates that adapter from
chronological interaction batches and retains a bounded personal memory.  This
note specifies their workflows, objectives, data models, inputs, outputs, and
the exact point where our agent-learning problem begins.
\end{abstract}
